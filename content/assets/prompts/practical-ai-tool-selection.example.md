## Cover image direction

Minimal editorial graphic using the Julio Vela palette.

Use:
- Signal Red `#E11D2E` as a small accent.
- Space Gray `#1F2329` for primary typography or shapes.
- Soft White `#F5F6F7` background.

Avoid:
- Robots
- Circuits
- Neon cyberpunk visuals
- Fake screenshots
- Imitating a living artist or brand style

## Prompt draft

Create a minimal premium editorial cover image for an article about choosing practical AI tools. Use a soft white background, space gray geometric blocks, and one signal red accent. The composition should feel clean, technical, and trustworthy, with no robots, circuits, neon, or fake UI screenshots.

## Alt text draft

Minimal abstract editorial graphic representing practical AI tool selection with geometric shapes in the Julio Vela brand palette.
