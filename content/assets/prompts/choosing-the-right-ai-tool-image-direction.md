## Preferred concept

Original editorial illustration:
- Soft White background with generous negative space.
- A restrained conceptual scene showing signal vs noise in tool selection.
- A Signal Red line begins at the left edge and moves toward a red circular decision node.
- Muted non-circular gray abstract tool tokens sit around subtle pencil-like exploratory paths.
- The red decision node is the only circle in the composition.
- No fake app UI, no screenshots, no robots, no circuits, no neon.
- Space reserved for Julio Vela logo lockup and article title if text is added in the OG layout.

This concept is preferred because it communicates the article's thesis visually: the right AI tool is the one that fits a real workflow, not the one that creates the most noise.

Composition:
1200x630 horizontal layout. Keep generous whitespace. Use a simple abstract illustration as the main visual idea. Leave safe space for the final minimal copy lockup: `:// Choosing the Right AI Tool` plus one short supporting line. The concept must remain readable at social preview size.

Include:
