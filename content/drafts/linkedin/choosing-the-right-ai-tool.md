The best AI tool is not always the newest one.

It is the one that improves a real workflow without adding unnecessary complexity.

Before adding another AI tool to your stack, I would ask:

1. What workflow are we trying to improve?
2. Does this reduce real friction?
3. Is the output reliable enough to use repeatedly?
4. What privacy or data questions does it create?
5. Is the value still there after the novelty wears off?

A good tool should earn its place.

Start with one workflow, one measurable outcome, and one honest test.

Soft CTA:
If you are evaluating AI tools, start with the workflow before comparing features.
