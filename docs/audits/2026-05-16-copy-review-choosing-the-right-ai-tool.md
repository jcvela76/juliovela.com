## Purpose

Apply the `copy-editor` review step to the first published article before making copy changes.

This audit is diagnostic only. It does not modify the article.

## Editorial Goal

Preserve the article's current intent:

> Help professionals and teams evaluate AI tools by workflow fit, reliability, privacy, cost, and long-term usefulness instead of hype.

The article should continue to sound:

- Practical
- Technical
- Clear
- Modern
- Trustworthy
- Non-hype

## Copy Review Summary

The article is strong as a first published piece. It is clear, useful, and aligned with the Julio Vela brand direction.

The main improvement opportunity is not a rewrite. The article needs a sharper opening and a stronger closing so readers immediately understand who it is for and what to do next.

Current strengths:

- Clear workflow-first point of view.
- No generic AI hype.
- No fake metrics, testimonials, or unsupported claims.
- Practical section structure.
- Useful evaluation criteria.
- Strong alignment with the homepage positioning.

Current gaps:

- Audience is implied but not explicit.
- The opening is useful but could state the reader context more directly.
- The closing is aligned but could end with a more actionable next step.
- Some section transitions could be slightly smoother, but this is not a blocker.

### Opening

Current opening:

> The best AI tool is not always the newest one, the loudest one, or the one with the longest feature list. It is the one that improves a real workflow without adding unnecessary complexity.

> Before adding another tool to your stack, slow down and evaluate whether it solves a clear problem, fits how you already work, and can be trusted with the information it needs.

Assessment:

This is a solid opening. It establishes anti-hype positioning and practical judgment. However, it does not explicitly say who the article is for.

Recommended addition before or after the first paragraph:

> This guide is for professionals and teams evaluating AI tools for real work, not demos, trends, or novelty.

Recommendation:

Add one audience/context sentence near the top. Do not over-expand the intro.

### Keep only what earns its place
