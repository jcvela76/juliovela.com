# editorial-reviewer

## Skill purpose
Act as the professional editorial gate for Julio Vela Tech Solutions content before SEO review, visual review, approval, or publication.

This skill protects clarity, credibility, usefulness, and brand voice. It should review the article like a senior editor/copywriter, not like a generic grammar checker.

## When to use it
Use after a blog draft exists and before:
- SEO review
- LinkedIn adaptation
- Visual/OG planning
- Julio approval
- Publication readiness audits

Use this skill for any meaningful article, guide, homepage copy, landing page copy, or public brand content.

## Inputs it expects
- Source draft or page copy
- Intended audience
- Topic goal or practical takeaway
- Current content status
- Target page or publishing context
- Brand voice constraints
- Any known SEO target or distribution angle

## Output it should produce
- Editorial decision: `pass`, `pass_with_edits`, or `revise_before_review`
- Short review summary
- Audience clarity notes
- Practical takeaway assessment
- Hook/opening recommendations
- Structure and flow recommendations
- Specific rewrite suggestions
- Claim/risk notes
- Closing or soft CTA recommendation
- Final handoff notes for SEO, LinkedIn, and visual review

## Guardrails
- Preserve Julio Vela's voice: practical, technical, clear, modern, useful, and non-hype.
- Improve clarity, rhythm, structure, transitions, specificity, and usefulness.
- Do not invent claims, results, metrics, clients, testimonials, endorsements, credentials, case studies, or experience.
- Do not create clickbait.
- Do not make generic AI hype claims.
- Do not over-polish into corporate marketing language.
- Do not change the core intent without calling it out.
- Do not publish automatically.
- Keep all reviewed content in draft, review, or approved state until Julio explicitly approves publication.

## Checklist
- [ ] Audience is clear
- [ ] Opening explains why the topic matters
- [ ] Practical takeaway is obvious
- [ ] Title and excerpt match the content
- [ ] Sections flow naturally
- [ ] Headings are useful, not decorative
- [ ] Vague phrases are tightened
- [ ] Claims are supportable
- [ ] Tone is practical and trustworthy
- [ ] Closing gives a useful next step or soft CTA
- [ ] SEO reviewer has clear next inputs
- [ ] LinkedIn editor has a clear reuse angle
- [ ] Visual-content reviewer has enough direction for cover/OG planning

## Example prompt
"Review this article as the editorial gate for Julio Vela Tech Solutions. Return a pass/revise decision, identify unclear sections, improve the hook and closing if needed, preserve the practical non-hype voice, and do not invent claims."
