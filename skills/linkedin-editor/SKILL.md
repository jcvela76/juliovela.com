# linkedin-editor

## Skill purpose
Convert blog/article ideas into native LinkedIn post drafts.

## When to use it
Use after a draft topic or article is available and needs social adaptation.

## Inputs it expects
- Source draft content
- Channel objective
- Preferred tone and CTA style

## Output it should produce
- LinkedIn-native draft with hook, body, and close
- Soft CTA
- Draft state marker (not final)

## Guardrails
- Strong hook and clear structure.
- Human, practical tone.
- Focus on practical insight.
- No clickbait.
- No fake claims.
- CTA must be soft.
- Never publish automatically.

## Checklist
- [ ] Hook is practical and specific
- [ ] Story is structured and readable
- [ ] No clickbait language
- [ ] No unsupported metrics/claims
- [ ] CTA is non-aggressive

## Example prompt
"Rewrite this article excerpt into a practical LinkedIn draft with a soft CTA and no hype."
